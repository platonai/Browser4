## What It Is

The **Wuhan Lobster Festival**, officially branded as the **"2026 Wuhan Crayfish Consumption Season" (2026武汉小龙虾消费季)**, is a large-scale, multi-month consumption promotion event organized by the **Wuhan Municipal Commerce Bureau** (武汉市商务局) in collaboration with the Municipal Science and Innovation Bureau and other departments. It is designed to:

- Extend the consumption momentum from Wuhan's spring cherry blossom season
- Leverage the May Day "Golden Week" holiday period
- Bridge through Dragon Boat Festival and summer vacation season
- Promote Wuhan's crayfish (小龙虾) food culture as a city brand and IP

The event frames crayfish as Wuhan's "summer-limited taste symbol" (夏日限定的味觉符号) and positions the city as an "international consumption center" (国际消费中心城市).

## History and Background

- **Origins**: Wuhan has long been known as China's "Crayfish City" (小龙虾之城). The formal, branded "Consumption Season" format began in recent years as a government-organized initiative, building on Wuhan's deep culinary tradition of crayfish dining and its position as China's largest crayfish consumption market.
- **2026 edition**: On April 21, 2026, the Commerce Bureau publicly solicited service platforms for distributing consumption vouchers. On April 28, the municipal government held a press conference to announce the event. The main venue carnival launched on April 30.
- **National context**: According to Meituan data, **Wuhan accounts for 41.9% of China's total crayfish consumption** — "out of every 10 crayfish sold in China, about 4 are consumed in Wuhan."
- **Supply chain**: Wuhan collaborates with **Qianjiang** (潜江), a major crayfish-producing region in Hubei, forming a "production + consumption" complementary model.

## Major Activities — Four Themed Series

The event is organized around four major series ("四大系列"):

1. **虾聚江城 (Crayfish Gathering Jiangcheng)** — Food-focused: crayfish food festivals, beer carnivals, crayfish markets, and promotion of famous dishes and restaurants. Features well-known local brands: 肥肥虾庄, 靓靓蒸虾, 巴厘龙虾, 一棠龙虾, 虾皇.

2. **展演江城 (Exhibition Jiangcheng)** — Integration of crayfish with exhibitions and events: Optics Valley Expo, Central China International Auto Show, Tea Expo, anime/gaming festivals, and sports event tie-ins (e.g., Chu Super League football).

3. **不夜江城 (Sleepless Jiangcheng)** — Nighttime economy: beer and music carnivals, Summer Solstice Music Day, summer lifestyle festivals, ice & fire parties, illustration art festivals, and multi-dimensional nighttime consumption scenarios.

4. **惠享江城 (Benefit Jiangcheng)** — Consumer incentives: **¥3 million in special crayfish consumption vouchers** distributed across 13 batches via Meituan (美团), Dianping (大众点评), and Douyin (抖音) from May 1 to July 31. Combined platform and merchant discounts, trade-in programs, and lucky invoice campaigns covering dining, shopping, accommodation, tourism, and entertainment.

**Workarounds Applied During Task:**

1. **htmlsnapshot failure**: The `htmlsnapshot` command failed on Baidu Baike with `ReferenceError: __pulsar_utils__ is not defined`. Workaround: Used accessibility-tree snapshots (`snapshot -v`) and `WebFetch` for content extraction.
2. **Off-screen element clicking**: The news article link was in a different viewport than what the accessibility tree exposed in viewport 0. Workaround: Used `WebFetch` to access content directly rather than fighting with viewport positioning.
3. **Tab management with `--follow`**: Baidu search result clicks open new tabs. The `--follow` flag worked correctly but created extra tabs that required manual tracking.
