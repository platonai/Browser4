## A. Task Result: Wuhan Lobster Festival (武汉龙虾节) Summary

Based on Baidu search results from multiple authoritative sources (Baidu Baike, 央广网/CNR, 21世纪经济报道/21st Century Business Herald, 湖北省农业农村厅/Hubei Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, 武汉市商务局/Wuhan Municipal Bureau of Commerce):

### What It Is

The **Wuhan Lobster Festival** — officially named **"2026武汉小龙虾消费季"** (2026 Wuhan Crayfish Consumption Season), with the theme **"江湖虾客 乐购武汉"** (River-Lake Shrimp Guests, Happy Shopping in Wuhan) — is a large-scale, city-wide consumption promotion event organized by the Wuhan Municipal Bureau of Commerce and the Municipal Science and Innovation Bureau. It was launched on **April 30, 2026** at **汉口江滩三阳广场** (Hankou Jiangtan Sanyang Square) and runs through **August 2026**.

### History and Background

- The event was designed to **extend the consumption momentum from Wuhan's Cherry Blossom Season**, leveraging the May Day Golden Week and linking Dragon Boat Festival, summer vacation, and other key commercial periods.
- The core strategy is **"商旅文体健联动"** (commerce-tourism-culture-sports-health integration), with four major series of "小龙虾+" (Crayfish+) themed consumption activities.
- The event adopts a **"1 main venue + 15 district sub-venues + N特色活动"** city-wide linkage model — all 15 districts of Wuhan participate simultaneously.
- This is a continuation and expansion of previous years' lobster festivals, with 2026 being a particularly ambitious edition targeting national and global audiences.

### Major Activities

1. **Giant Steamer Free Tasting** — A massive communal steamer at the main venue offers free crayfish samples to visitors.
2. **Six Themed Markets & Music Carnival** — At the main venue (三阳广场), featuring food, crafts, and live music.
3. **Beer & Crayfish Carnival** — Held at Huangpi Qijiawan (黄陂祁家湾), drawing over 50,000 visitors in a single day, with 6,000 jin (~3,000 kg) of crayfish served.
4. **Sports & Auto Shows** — 楚超 (Chu Super League) live broadcasts and the Optical Expo / Central China International Auto Show are bundled into the experience.
5. **Nighttime Economy** — Crayfish eating peaks at night (44% of daily orders during evening hours), complemented by river-view night scenery, old-street ambiance, and business district access for "夜食、夜游、夜娱、夜购" (night dining, touring, entertainment, shopping).
6. **¥3 Million Crayfish-Specific Consumption Vouchers** — Distributed across 13 batches via Meituan, Dianping, and Douyin platforms, with brand-specific discounts (肥肥虾庄 46-store universal coupons, 靓靓蒸虾 20%+ off sets, 巴厘龙虾 up to 50% off individual items). These are stackable with platform coupons.
7. **E-Commerce Synergy** — Taobao/Tmall launched "寻味山河行｜小龙虾节·武汉站", Douyin invested over ¥1 million in subsidies for "烟火武汉龙虾季", and Meituan launched "大众点评小龙虾打卡地图".
8. **Over 800 crayfish brand restaurants** city-wide participate, including major chains: 肥肥虾庄, 靓靓蒸虾, 巴厘龙虾, 一棠龙虾, 虾皇, etc.
9. **Cross-city linkage** with Qianjiang City (潜江市), creating a "production origin + consumption destination" complementary model.

### Typical Schedule and Venue

- **Period:** Late April to August (2026 edition: April 30 – August)
- **Main Venue:** 汉口江滩三阳广场, Wuhan
- **Sub-Venues:** 15 districts across Wuhan
- **Coupon Period:** May 1 – July 31 (13 batches)
- **Key dates:** Launch on April 30 (before May Day Golden Week); ongoing activities through summer

### Significance

- **Tourism:** Positions Wuhan as a must-visit crayfish destination with the slogan "来武汉、嘬虾子" (Come to Wuhan, eat crayfish), attracting visitors nationally and globally.
- **Food Culture:** Wuhan has established itself as a national leader in crayfish consumption — described as "全国第一" (No. 1 nationwide). Crayfish eating is deeply embedded in Wuhan's nighttime culture, with the city's famous "虾城" (shrimp city) identity.
- **Economy:** The event functions as a **city IP engine**, driving consumer spending across food service, hospitality, retail, and entertainment. It leverages digital platforms (Meituan, Douyin, Taobao) for both promotion and subsidy distribution, creating a data-driven consumption ecosystem. The ¥3 million voucher program is designed to multiply into significantly larger economic activity.
- **Industry Development:** Strengthens the Hubei crayfish industrial chain, connecting rural producers (especially Qianjiang) with urban consumers, and promoting the "湖北小龙虾" (Hubei Crayfish) brand at a national level.

### Key Decisions

- **Used accessibility snapshot for interaction** (`snapshot -v 0`) to find element refs for the search form — this worked well per the documented core loop.
- **Used HTML snapshot for extraction** (`htmlsnapshot` + `htmlsnapshot get text`) rather than clicking individual result links — this proved much more efficient, extracting AI-generated Baidu summaries that already synthesized information across 29 sources.
- **Skipped clicking individual result links** because the Baidu AI summary already provided comprehensive, multi-source information covering all required aspects of the task.

### Workarounds Required

- **Baidu search results page format:** The page is very dense with ads, images, sidebars, and formatted result cards. The `snapshot -i` interactive output was extremely verbose. Had to use `snapshot grep` to find relevant elements.
- **`summarize` command returned no useful content:** The AI summary indicated it only received CSS/JavaScript styling code, not the actual page content. This may be an LLM integration issue or a page complexity issue.
- **Pagination awareness:** The snapshot showed `viewportsTotal: 4` with `hiddenBottomHeight: 2215px` — meaning most content was below the fold. Only viewport 0 was captured.

## C. Issues Found

### Issue 1: `summarize` command returns empty/useless output on complex pages

**Severity:** High

**Category:** Reliability

**Reproduction:**
```
cd cli/browser4-cli && cargo run -- goto "https://www.baidu.com/s?wd=武汉龙虾节"
cd cli/browser4-cli && cargo run -- summarize "What is the Wuhan Lobster Festival?"
```
Read the output file to see: "I cannot provide a summary... the content you pasted consists entirely of website styling code (CSS and JavaScript) and contains no information about the festival."

**Expected:** The AI should extract and summarize the visible text content of the search results page.

**Actual:** The LLM received only CSS/JavaScript code and returned an error message stating no content was available.

**Root Cause:** The `summarize` command likely sends raw HTML (including all CSS and JS) to the LLM rather than extracting and sending only the visible text content. On a complex page like Baidu search results (~1515 KB HTML), the LLM receives too much noise and too little signal. The `htmlsnapshot get text` command, by contrast, successfully extracted rich text content.

**Code Pointer:** Backend Java/Kotlin code handling the `summarize` MCP tool — likely the summarization pipeline that prepares content for the LLM.

**AI Suggested Improvement:**
- Use the same text extraction pipeline as `htmlsnapshot get text` to pre-process content before sending to the LLM for summarization
- Strip `<style>` and `<script>` tags before sending HTML to the LLM
- Consider using the WPSI (Web Page Summary Index) from `htmlsnapshot summary` as input for LLM summarization instead of raw HTML
- Add a `--selector` option to `summarize` to scope the content sent to the LLM, matching the `snapshot -s` / `htmlsnapshot get` pattern

### Issue 3: `htmlsnapshot get text` with compound CSS selector returns only one match

**Severity:** Low

**Category:** Product

**Reproduction:**
```
cd cli/browser4-cli && cargo run -- htmlsnapshot get text "h3 a, .cosc-title-a" --all
```
Expected to return all matching elements (multiple result titles). Actually returned only one match: the Baidu Baike title.

**Expected:** The `--all` flag combined with `get` (not `get all`) should either work, or the CLI should warn that `get` only returns the first match regardless of `--all`.

**Actual:** The `--all` flag on `get` is silently accepted but only returns the first match. Users need to use `get all` (a separate subcommand) for querySelectorAll semantics.

**Root Cause:** The `--all` flag on `htmlsnapshot get` controls output pagination (disable line truncation), not element matching behavior. This is documented but the naming is confusing — `--all` on `get` means "show all output lines" while `get all` means "match all elements". These are easily conflated.

**Code Pointer:** `cli/browser4-cli/src/args.rs` — argument definition for `htmlsnapshot get`.

**AI Suggested Improvement:**
- Rename `--all` pagination flag to `--no-pagination` or `--full` to avoid confusion with `get all`
- When `--all` is passed to `get` (not `get all`), emit a hint: "💡 Tip: `get` returns only the first match. Use `get all` for querySelectorAll semantics."

### Issue 6: No built-in search engine integration for common search tasks

**Severity:** Medium

**Category:** Discoverability / Product

**Reproduction:** A user wanting to "search Baidu for X" must manually:
1. `goto` to baidu.com
2. `snapshot` to find the search box ref
3. `fill` the search box
4. `click` the search button
5. `wait` for results

There's no `search` command or search-engine abstraction.

**Expected:** A higher-level command like `search "武汉龙虾节" --engine baidu` or a documented pattern/recipe for web search that abstracts the engine-specific steps.

**Root Cause:** browser4-cli is designed as a general-purpose browser automation tool, not a search-focused tool. However, web search is the most common starting point for research tasks.

**AI Suggested Improvement:**
- Add a `search <query>` command that handles the common search engines (Baidu, Google, Bing) with a `--engine` flag
- At minimum, add a "Search Pattern" recipe to SKILL.md showing the exact workflow for Baidu and Google search
