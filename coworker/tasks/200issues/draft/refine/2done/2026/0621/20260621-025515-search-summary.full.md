# A. Task Result — Wuhan Lobster Festival (武汉龙虾节)

## Official Name and Identity

The event's official name is **2026武汉小龙虾消费季** (2026 Wuhan Crawfish Consumption Season). It is a government-led consumption promotion initiative organized by the **Wuhan Municipal Commerce Bureau (武汉市商务局)** in collaboration with the Municipal Science and Innovation Bureau (市科创局) and other departments.

## What the Wuhan Lobster Festival Is

The Wuhan Lobster Festival is a city-wide, multi-month crawfish (crayfish/small lobster) food and consumption festival. It is not a single-day event or a privately organized fair — it is a coordinated municipal campaign that combines food tourism, technology exhibitions, live entertainment, sports events, and e-commerce into a comprehensive "小龙虾+" (Crawfish+) consumption ecosystem. The 2026 edition is referred to in slogans as "全城嘬虾" (Whole City Sucks Shrimp — "嘬" being the local verb for eating crawfish).

## History and Background

- The event is relatively young. Based on search results, organized municipal-scale Wuhan lobster festivals date back to at least **2024–2025**. A 2025 article from Guangming Online references the "武汉小龙虾消费季" attracting over a million visitors as early as May 2025.
- The 2026 edition marks a significant expansion: it is structured as **four major series** of "小龙虾+" consumption promotion activities spanning April through August.
- The event is intentionally scheduled to **bridge multiple holiday seasons**: building on consumption momentum from the cherry blossom season (March), leveraging the May Day Golden Week (五一), connecting through Dragon Boat Festival (端午), and extending into the summer vacation period.
- The Baidu Baike entry has dedicated sections for "历史沿革" (Historical Development) and "活动背景" (Activity Background), indicating institutional recognition.
- The broader tradition of lobster festivals in China is well established — the Xuyi Lobster Festival (盱眙龙虾节) in Jiangsu has been running for over 20 years. Wuhan's event is the city's adaptation of this model to its own renowned crawfish cuisine culture.

## Major Activities

1. **Six Themed Markets (六大主题市集)** at the main venue:
   - **Crawfish Gourmet Zone**: Top Wuhan brands (肥肥虾庄, 靓靓蒸虾, 巴厘龙虾, 一棠龙虾, 虾皇, 潜江龙虾, 周黑鸭"聚一虾") with diverse cooking styles (油焖, 清蒸, 蒜蓉, etc.)
   - **E-commerce & Interactive Zone**: Live-streamed sales on Douyin/Meituan with flash deals
   - **District Specialties Zone**: Each of Wuhan's 15 districts brings local foods (热干面, 三鲜豆皮, 面窝, 鸭脖, 武昌鱼, 黄陂三鲜, etc.)
   - **Science & Innovation Zone**: Technology experiences including brain-computer interface (脑机接口) demonstrations
   - **Foreign Trade Products Zone**: Imported quality goods
   - **Trade-in Zone**: Consumer goods replacement programs

2. **Music Carnivals**: Five music carnival evenings during May Day, combined with "楚超" (Chu Super League) football match broadcasts — creating a "watch football, eat crawfish" atmosphere.

3. **Three Theme Parades**: Costume and cultural parades through the venue.

4. **Free Tasting Events**: The opening ceremony featured a 3-meter diameter giant steamer distributing 10,000+ free crawfish. Wuhan College held its 3rd campus lobster festival with a 2.7m giant pot cooking 60,000+ crawfish free for students.

5. **Technology Interactions**: Brain-computer interface games (brainwave-controlled cotton candy machines, racing), smart robot appearances.

6. **Consumption Vouchers**: 3 million RMB in special crawfish vouchers distributed through Meituan, Dianping, and Douyin platforms (valid through July 31). Additional 250,000 RMB in on-site vouchers during May Day.
