# A. Task Result: 武汉龙虾节 (Wuhan Lobster Festival) — Comprehensive Summary

## What It Is

**武汉龙虾节** (officially named **"2026武汉小龙虾消费季"** — 2026 Wuhan Crayfish Consumption Season) is a large-scale, city-wide consumer promotion event organized by the **Wuhan Municipal Commerce Bureau** (武汉市商务局), in coordination with the Municipal Science and Innovation Bureau and other departments. The official theme is **"江湖虾客 乐购武汉"** ("Rivers and Lakes Shrimp Guests, Happy Shopping in Wuhan"). It is a months-long festival centered on Wuhan's signature food: crayfish (小龙虾).

The festival is not a single-day event but a sustained campaign running from **April to August 2026**, designed to extend the consumption boom after Wuhan's famous cherry blossom season, leveraging the May Day Golden Week, and connecting through Dragon Boat Festival and summer holidays. It integrates commerce, tourism, culture, sports, and technology.

## History and Background

- **Origins**: The concept builds on Wuhan's established dominance in China's crayfish market. According to a Meituan consumption report, **Wuhan accounts for 41.9% of national crayfish consumption** — meaning roughly 4 out of every 10 crayfish sold in China are consumed in Wuhan. The city has the highest crayfish consumption scale, restaurant density, and per-capita consumption frequency nationwide.
- **2026 Planning**: On April 21, 2026, the Wuhan Commerce Bureau began publicly soliciting service platforms to distribute crayfish consumption vouchers. A press conference was held on April 28 to announce details. The event officially launched on April 30, 2026.
- **Evolution**: While Wuhan has long had informal "lobster seasons" driven by its famous crayfish restaurant brands, the 2026 edition represents the most formally organized and government-backed iteration, with a unified city-wide framework ("1+15+N") and significant public investment (¥3 million in vouchers).

## Major Activities

The festival is organized around **four thematic series**:

1. **"虾聚江城" (Shrimp Gathering in the River City)**: Food-focused events — crayfish food festivals, beer carnivals, lobster markets, and famous dish competitions, partnered with major restaurant brands.

2. **"展演江城" (Exhibition & Performance)**: Integration with major exhibitions (Optics Valley Expo, Central China Auto Show, Tea Expo) and events (anime/gaming festivals), plus sports tie-ins with the **"楚超" (Chu Super League)** football matches.

3. **"不夜江城" (Nighttime River City)**: Night economy events — beer & music carnivals, Summer Solstice Music Day, lifestyle festivals, and traditional-meets-modern ("国潮") events integrating music, food, and night tours.

4. **"惠享江城" (Benefits for the River City)**: Consumer benefits — **¥3 million in crayfish-specific consumption vouchers** distributed in 13 batches via Meituan, Dianping, and Douyin (TikTok) platforms (May 1 – July 31). Also includes trade-in programs and invoice lottery promotions.

**Signature experiences** include:
- A **7-square-meter (3m+ diameter) giant steaming pot** cooking 10,000 crayfish for free public tasting at launch
- **6 themed markets**: brand exhibition, tech consumption, district specialties, livestream check-in, export goods, and trade-in zones
- **AI digital mascot "钳宝" (Qian Bao)** — an AI-powered crayfish IP character unveiled at the launch ceremony
- **"一路钳行" (All the Way with Claws) global crayfish promotion plan**, jointly launched with Qianjiang city (a major crayfish-producing region)
- **Night music performances** for 5 consecutive nights during May Day with interactive lucky draws giving away ¥250,000 in on-site "嘬虾券" (shrimp-eating vouchers)
- **15 district-level sub-venues** each hosting localized events

## Issue 2: Commands unexpectedly execute as background tasks

**Severity:** High
**Category:** UX / Reliability

**Reproduction:** Run `browser4-cli fill e204 "武汉龙虾节"` or `browser4-cli click e538`. Both commands spawned as background tasks (`Command running in background with ID: ...`) rather than executing synchronously.

**Expected:** Commands should execute synchronously and return their output directly, as documented in SKILL.md. The documentation shows inline output patterns like:
```
### Page
- Page URL: https://...
### Snapshot
[Snapshot](...)
```

**Actual:** Commands ran in background, requiring the user to poll `TaskOutput` to get results. This adds significant friction — every interaction becomes a two-step process (run command → poll for result).

**Why this matters:** The SKILL.md documentation implies synchronous execution. A first-time user would be confused by the background task behavior and wouldn't know to use `TaskOutput` to retrieve results. The task flow is dramatically slower and more cumbersome than expected.

**Suggested Improvement:** Investigate why the harness is running these commands as background tasks. Either fix the harness behavior, or document this clearly in SKILL.md with explicit instructions on how to retrieve background task output.

## Issue 3: Clicking Baidu search result links silently fails (JS redirect tracking)

**Severity:** High
**Category:** Product / Reliability

**Reproduction:**
1. Perform a Baidu search
2. Snapshot results and identify a result link ref (e.g., `e11234` for the Baike entry)
3. Run `browser4-cli click e11234`
4. Observe that the page URL and content remain on the search results page

**Expected:** Clicking a search result link should navigate to the target page.

**Actual:** The snapshot reports the same search results page URL. Baidu uses `baidu.com/link?url=...` redirect URLs with JavaScript tracking. The accessibility-tree click appears to trigger the click event, but the JS-based navigation doesn't complete — the browser stays on the search results page.

**Workaround:** Manually construct and navigate to the target URL using `goto`.

**Suggested Improvement:** Consider whether browser4-cli's click implementation properly handles pages that use JavaScript `window.location` or `window.open` for navigation rather than standard `<a href>` navigation.

## Issue 4: `extract` command times out (30s MCP endpoint timeout)

**Severity:** Medium
**Category:** Reliability / Product

**Reproduction:**
1. Navigate to a content-heavy page (e.g., Baidu Baike)
2. Run `browser4-cli extract "Extract all text content about..."` with a descriptive prompt

**Expected:** Structured extraction of page content based on the instruction.

**Actual:** `Error: HTTP request timed out [tool=agent_extract, endpoint=http://localhost:8182/mcp/call-tool, timeout=30s]`

**Workaround:** Use `browser4-cli eval` with raw JavaScript to extract text content.

**Suggested Improvement:** Increase the default timeout for the extract command, or allow users to pass a `--timeout` parameter. The 30-second limit is too short for AI-powered extraction on content-rich pages.
