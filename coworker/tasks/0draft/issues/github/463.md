# Article content is truncated in snapshot due to lazy-loading or viewport-only capture

URL: https://github.com/platonai/Browser4/issues/463
State: OPEN
Author: galaxyeye
Created: 06/21/2026 03:04:05
Updated: 06/21/2026 03:04:05

When navigating to a long article page (e.g., a Baidu Baijiahao article) and taking a snapshot, only the visible portion of the article is captured. Content below the initial viewport is omitted, likely because the accessibility tree only includes elements within the current viewport, or because the page uses lazy-loading for off-screen content.

## Steps to reproduce

1. Navigate to a Baidu Baijiahao article or similar long-form page
2. Run `browser4-cli snapshot`
3. Read the snapshot file

## Expected behavior

The full article body is captured in the snapshot.

## Actual behavior

The snapshot captures the article header and first few paragraphs, then cuts off — showing UI elements like a comment box ("发表神评妙论") and "作者最新文章" (author's latest articles) that normally appear at the bottom of the page, while the middle of the article body is missing.

## Additional context

- The truncation is likely caused by lazy-loading or the accessibility tree only containing viewport-visible content at the time of capture.
- Mitigations could include: auto-scrolling through the page before capturing, or providing a "full-page snapshot" option that scrolls and aggregates content.
- This significantly reduces the value of `snapshot` for research tasks that require reading full articles.
