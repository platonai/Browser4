### Issue 3: No automatic re-snapshot after navigation — silent ref staleness risk

**Root Cause:** After navigation events (`goto`, `go-back`, `reload`, tab switches), the DOM is rebuilt and CDP backend node IDs change. **Code Pointer:** `cli/browser4-cli/src/commands.rs` (command dispatch layer — could track last navigation event and warn)

- Add an `--auto-snapshot` flag to `click`, `fill`, and other interaction commands that automatically re-snapshots before interacting
