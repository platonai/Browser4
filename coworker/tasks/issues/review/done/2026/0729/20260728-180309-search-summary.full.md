## A. Task Result — Wuhan Lobster Festival (武汉龙虾节) Summary

### What the Wuhan Lobster Festival Is

The "Wuhan Lobster Festival" is not a single event but rather a constellation of city-wide **crayfish (小龙虾) consumption promotion activities** organized under the umbrella brand **"武汉小龙虾消费季"** (Wuhan Crayfish Consumption Season). The flagship event is a large-scale carnival held at **汉口江滩三阳广场** (Hankou River Beach Sanyang Square), complemented by numerous regional sub-festivals across Wuhan's districts. The official organizing body is the **武汉市商务局** (Wuhan Municipal Commerce Bureau), which brands the season with the slogan "不嘬虾，枉夏天" (If you don't eat crayfish, you've wasted your summer).

### History and Background

- **2024**: The earliest documented iteration — Wuhan College held its first lobster festival with a "farewell graduating students" theme.
- **2025**: Expanded into a cultural immersion theme (荆楚文化浸润) at Wuhan College. The city-level "夏日荟聚" consumption promotion + crayfish consumption season launched, attracting over **1 million visitors** to the Hankou river beach main venue.
- **2026**: Major scale-up. The Wuhan College festival entered its **third year** with AI/robot integration. The Qijiawan (祁家湾) festival entered its **second year** as "祁遇季" (Qi Encounter Season), evolving from a barbecue festival. The city-level carnival added brain-computer interface tech, AI digital human mascots, dual-city collaboration with Qianjiang (潜江), and simultaneous release of crayfish consumption reports by Meituan/Douyin/Taobao/Tmall.

The festivals are politically and economically significant — they are part of Wuhan's broader strategy to stimulate domestic consumption, promote agricultural supply chains (Qianjiang is China's crayfish capital), and build Wuhan's brand as a "crayfish city."
