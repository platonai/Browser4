# Use Case 12: Company Due Diligence Automation
# Level: Enterprise
# Type: Cross-site, long-running, auditable
# Description: Automated company due diligence report generation for Stripe

1. go to https://stripe.com/
2. extract product description and target customers from the homepage and /use-cases
3. go to https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/stripe
4. extract funding history and key investors (if Crunchbase blocks unauthenticated access, search "Stripe Crunchbase" on Google instead)
5. search "Stripe latest news" on Google News
6. open the latest 5 articles
7. identify major business events (funding rounds, acquisitions, product launches, regulatory actions)
8. assess risk and maturity from the collected data
9. generate a due diligence report as `stripe-due-diligence.md`
