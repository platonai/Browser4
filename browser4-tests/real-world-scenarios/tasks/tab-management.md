# tab-management

1. Go to `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_scraping`.
2. List the currently open tabs to see the initial state.
3. Open a new tab and navigate to `https://news.ycombinator.com`.
4. Open another new tab and navigate to `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_mining`.
5. List all tabs to see the three open tabs with their indices.
6. Switch to the first tab (the Web scraping article) by its index, and take a snapshot to verify the context is correct.
7. Switch to the Hacker News tab by its index, and take a snapshot to verify the context switched correctly.
8. Close the Hacker News tab.
9. List tabs again to confirm it was removed.
10. Switch back to the remaining tabs and verify each still works by taking a snapshot.
