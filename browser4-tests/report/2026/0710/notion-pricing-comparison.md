## Tier-by-Tier Breakdown

### 🆓 Free — $0 per member / month

For individuals to organize personal projects and life.

**Core Features:**
- Trial AI capabilities (generate docs, autofill databases)
- Capture, organize, and act on responses inside Notion
- Basic forms — publish a Notion page to the web
- Basic sites
- Notion Calendar — connect multiple Calendars
- Databases with subtasks, dependencies, and custom properties

**Limits:**
- Pages & blocks: Unlimited for individuals; limited for 2+ members
- File uploads: Up to 5 MB each
- Page history: 7 days
- Charts: 1
- Guest limit: 10
- Automations: Basic buttons only
- SEO: Basic indexing
- notion.site domains: 1
- Offline: Choose pages to download

---

### ➕ Plus* — $10 per member / month

For small teams and professionals to work together.

**Everything in Free, plus:**

- Trial of Notion AI
- **Custom forms** — remove Notion branding from public forms
- **Custom sites** — customize header, theme, favicon, Google Analytics integration
- **Unlimited charts** — donut, bar, line charts from database data
- **Unlimited collaborative blocks**
- **Unlimited file uploads**
- **Basic connections** — Slack, Google Drive

**Upgraded Limits:**
- Pages & blocks: Unlimited
- File uploads: Unlimited (~5 GB max per file)
- Page history: 30 days
- Guest limit: Unlimited
- Automations: Custom database automations
- SEO: Advanced
- notion.site domains: 5
- Offline: Recents & Favorites auto-download
- Custom domains & branding: $8/mo/domain (annual) or $10/mo/domain (monthly)

---

### 🏢 Business (Recommended) — $20 per member / month

For growing businesses to streamline teamwork.

**Everything in Plus, plus:**

- **Notion Agent** — completes complex, multi-step tasks using context from Notion, connected apps, and the web
- **AI Meeting Notes** — automate meeting notes and follow-ups, no bot needed
- **Enterprise Search (Beta)** — search across connected apps (Slack, GitHub, etc.)
- **SAML SSO** — manage employee access with secure single sign-on
- **Granular database permissions** — limit row-level access by assignee
- **Verify any page** — add verified badges, appears in search & AI citations
- **Private teamspaces** — only visible to added members
- **Domain verification** — verify email domain ownership
- **Premium connections** — GitHub, Asana, and more

**Upgraded Limits:**
- Page history: 90 days
- Forms: Custom + conditional logic
- Export: Entire workspace as HTML, Markdown, CSV
- 2-step verification (2FA/MFA)
- Workspace analytics
- Granular admin roles

---

### 🏛️ Enterprise* — Custom pricing

For organizations to operate with scalability, control, and security.

**Everything in Business, plus:**

- **Zero data retention** with LLM providers (when using Notion AI)
- **User provisioning (SCIM)** — provision and manage users & groups
- **Advanced security & controls** — restrict external sharing, disable guests, workspace-level rules
- **Audit log** — detailed security and safety activity log
- **Customer Success Manager** — dedicated CSM
- **Security & Compliance connections** — SIEM, DLP integrations
- **Domain management** — ownership actions on verified domain workspaces
- **Advanced connections** — connect to even more tools

**Upgraded Limits:**
- Page history: Unlimited
- Export: Entire workspace as PDF
- Managed users dashboard & controls
- Workspace consolidation
- Guest invite requests
- Custom data retention settings
- AI Meeting Notes transcript deletion
- Premium support
- Organization-level security controls

## Key Differentiators

1. **Free → Plus:** The jump from individual use to team collaboration. Removes block/file limits, adds unlimited guests, custom branding on forms/sites, and basic integrations.

2. **Plus → Business:** The "serious business" tier. Adds Notion Agent (AI), SAML SSO, private teamspaces, granular permissions, page verification, and 90-day page history.

3. **Business → Enterprise:** The governance and compliance tier. Adds zero-retention AI, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, SIEM/DLP integrations, domain management, custom data retention, and dedicated support.
