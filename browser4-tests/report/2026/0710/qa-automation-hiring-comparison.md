## 4. Demand Assessment

### Volume
- **Wellfound has dramatically higher volume** — 83 US QA Automation Engineer listings vs. ~2 on RemoteOK.
- Wellfound also lists international positions across 7+ countries.
- RemoteOK's QA listings appear dominated by a single employer (Mex Digital FZE / MultiBank Group).

### Industry Distribution
- **Wellfound:** Broader industry distribution — health tech (Qventus, Arya Health, Mochi Health), fintech (SuperDNA), analytics (Similarweb, Composable Analytics), AI (Whiterabbit.ai), gaming (ASAR Gaming), and legal tech (Kalmus).
- **RemoteOK:** Concentrated in fintech/trading (MultiBank Group), with incidental tech/DevOps roles appearing in QA-related searches.

### Seniority Mix
- **Wellfound:** Wide range from junior ($30k) to senior/lead ($190k + equity). 3+ years experience common for mid-level, 5+ for senior.
- **RemoteOK:** Mid-to-senior focus (3+ years required, fintech experience preferred).

### Emerging Trends (Both Platforms)
1. **Full-stack quality engineering** — QA roles increasingly require cloud, infrastructure, and observability skills.
2. **AI-assisted testing** — Adoption of AI tools (Cursor, ChatGPT) in QA workflows.
3. **Shift-left testing** — QA engineers expected to work closely with developers in CI/CD pipelines.
4. **Serverless/event-driven testing** — Growing demand for distributed systems testing expertise.

| **Skill breadth** | Narrower, role-specific | Broader, includes cloud/infra |
| **Job freshness** | Some listings 5+ months old | Typically more recent |
| **Geographic diversity** | Truly global remote | US-centric with growing intl. |
| **Bot accessibility** | Unrestricted | Blocked by DataDome CAPTCHA |

---

## 6. Recommendations for Job Seekers

1. **Use both platforms** — RemoteOK for niche global-remote roles, Wellfound for startup opportunities with equity upside.
2. **Budget for RemoteOK Premium** if salary data is critical — most listings hide compensation.
3. **Highlight full-stack quality engineering skills** — cloud, observability, and AI-assisted testing are increasingly expected.
4. **Consider equity as part of total compensation** — Wellfound listings frequently include meaningful equity stakes.
5. **Target fintech for premium QA roles** — trading/financial systems testing commands higher salaries and specialized demand.

---

## 7. Limitations of This Analysis

- Wellfound data sourced via web search due to DataDome CAPTCHA blocking automated browser access. Actual current listings may differ.
- RemoteOK salary data is incomplete due to premium paywall.
- Job listing counts reflect a single point-in-time snapshot (2026-07-10).
- Skill requirements extracted from job descriptions may not capture all implicit expectations.
